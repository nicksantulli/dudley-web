EconByte: Daily Economics Support

Learn economics in bite-sized daily cards — like a smart friend, not a textbook

Below are answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? Email us with your device and iOS version, the screen you were on, and what happened — a screenshot or recording helps.

Email Support

I'm seeing ads — how do I remove them?

Open Settings and tap Remove Ads ($0.99, one-time). After purchase, ads disappear permanently. If you already purchased, tap Restore Purchases in Settings to reactivate on this device. Note that Remove Ads is separate from Unlock All Topics — buying one doesn't unlock or remove the other.

I paid for Unlock All Topics but a topic is still locked.

Unlock All Topics opens all 13 paid topics at once — there's no per-topic purchase. If a topic still shows locked after buying, force-quit and reopen the app, then check Settings → Restore Purchases. If it's still locked after that, contact support at dudleyapps.com/support with your purchase receipt.

My streak reset unexpectedly.

A streak day is credited when you complete a daily set. The streak resets if you miss a full calendar day. Check your device date is correct: Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically. If there's a technical issue, contact support at dudleyapps.com/support.

My daily set looks the same as yesterday.

The daily set rotates each calendar day based on cards you haven't seen recently. After completing today's set, tomorrow's set will differ.

My bookmarks disappeared.

Bookmarks are stored locally on your device. Reinstalling the app clears local data — there's no account or cloud backup by design. Long-press any card to re-bookmark it.

How do I turn on or off the daily reminder, analytics, or diagnostics?

All three are opt-in and off by default. Go to Settings to turn on the daily reminder, or the optional usage-analytics and crash-diagnostics toggles, at any time. Turning them off again stops them immediately.

Still need a hand?

Email support@dudleyapps.com, or use the contact form — either way, it reaches the same inbox and we'll get back to you.
