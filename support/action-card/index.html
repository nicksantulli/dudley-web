ActionCard: Next Steps Support

Who does what by when — captured before the conversation ends.

Below are answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? Email us with your device and iOS version, the screen you were on, and what happened — a screenshot or recording helps.

Email Support

Where are my actions stored?

Actions, owner names, dates, notes, and completion state are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes that local data, so share anything you need to keep first.

How do I reopen or duplicate an action?

Open the action and use its controls to mark it open again or duplicate it. A duplicate gets a new identity and can be edited without changing the original.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings and choose Remove Ads. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Use Restore Purchases on another device signed into the same Apple Account.

Why am I not seeing ads?

ActionCard intentionally sends no ad requests during the first session, in restricted regions, or when the region is unknown. It also limits full-screen ads to natural breaks and caps how often they can appear.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Email support@dudleyapps.com with a screenshot and the approximate time it appeared. Never tap an ad just to identify it.

How do I contact support?

Email support@dudleyapps.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, the screen you were on, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps.

Still need a hand?

Email support@dudleyapps.com, or use the contact form — either way, it reaches the same inbox and we'll get back to you.
