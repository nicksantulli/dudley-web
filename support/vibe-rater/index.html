VibeRater Social Support

Your vibe, your people, your terms

Below are answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? Email us with your device and iOS version, the screen you were on, and what happened — a screenshot or recording helps.

Email Support

Is the rating real?

No. VibeRater is for entertainment. Scores, auras, archetypes, and Vibescopes are playful outputs, not measurements of you or anyone in a photo.

Where do my photos go?

A photo you only rate is processed in memory by the rating service and discarded. Media you choose to post, share, or save is stored with the related social data so that feature works. See the privacy policy for the full breakdown.

How do I delete my VibeRater account?

Open Settings in VibeRater and choose Delete Account. This removes your profile and associated social content and anonymizes your identifiers. You can also delete individual posts without deleting your account.

What does SlopGuard do?

SlopGuard can hide non-rated image posts that are flagged as suspected AI-generated. It is optional and can be changed in Settings.

What are VibeBoosts and VibeTokens?

They are optional, for-fun extras. VibeBoosts add a fixed +8 boost, while fixed-quantity VibeToken packs are used for fixed post-boost tiers. They have no cash value, prizes, or randomized rewards.

What is a Repli and how do I set one up?

Your Repli is your customizable avatar and room. Open the Repli tab to style your look, decorate your room, add rooms, choose a pet, and browse VibeShop for items you can try on before buying.

The phone vibrates while it analyzes — is that a bug?

Nope, that's the joke. It's a VibeRater, so it vibrates. You can mute haptics in Settings.
