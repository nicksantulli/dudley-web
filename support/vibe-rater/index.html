Common questions

5 support topics for VibeRater: Aura

Is the rating real?

VibeRater is for entertainment. It's a playful, for-fun take on your photo's vibe — not a measurement of anything real about you.

Where do my photos go?

When you pick a photo, it's sent to our rating service only to generate your vibe, then immediately discarded — it's processed in memory and never stored, retained, or tied to you. See the VibeRater privacy policy for details.

The phone vibrates while it analyzes — is that a bug?

Nope, that's the joke. It's a VibeRater, so it vibrates. You can mute haptics in Settings.

How do I clear my saved results?

Your recent results and saved vibe cards live on your device only. Clear them in the app's Settings, or remove everything by deleting the app.

Why does the website mention VibeRater Social?

VibeRater Social is the next version of VibeRater. The app you can download today is VibeRater: Aura; VibeRater Social adds Vibies, Squad, Radar, MiniVibe, and SlopGuard.
