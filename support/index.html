Fastest answer

Send the details that make bugs reproducible.

01

Name the app and screen

Tell us where it happened: result, settings, share card, level, purchase screen, deck, or daily card.

02

Include device context

iPhone model, iOS version, app version when available, and whether it happens every time.

03

Attach one clear artifact

A screenshot or short screen recording usually gives us the missing clue faster than a long paragraph.
