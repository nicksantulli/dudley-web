Common questions

The game is stuck on a level / something broke.

Tap the gear icon (top-right of the home screen) → Settings → Reset all progress. That clears saved state, unlock progress, and tutorial flags. Force-quit the app and reopen.

How do I unlock the later levels?

Beat each stage to unlock the next, all the way to Level 100. Hard ramps up; switch to Easy in Settings if you need a breather.

My Game Center score isn't showing.

Make sure you're signed into Apple's Game Center: iOS Settings → Game Center → sign in, then open the app → Settings → Leaderboards. Unsigned play still saves locally.

Can I turn off sound or haptics?

Yes. Gear icon → Settings → Audio & Feel. Sound effects, music, and haptics each toggle independently.

Is this endorsing or attacking a real person?

No. It's a parody with fictional characters; the app makes no political endorsement or statement.

Still stuck?

Email support@dudleyapps.com with the app name, iPhone model, iOS version, the screen where it happened, and a screenshot or short recording. That gives us enough signal to reproduce the issue quickly.
