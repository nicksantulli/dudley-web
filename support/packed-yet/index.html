Packed Yet? Trip Checklist Support

Pack once. Leave without the last-minute doubt.

Below are answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? Email us with your device and iOS version, the screen you were on, and what happened — a screenshot or recording helps.

Email Support

Where are my trips stored?

Trips and checklist items are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes that local data, so share any list you need to keep before removing the app.

How do I reuse an old trip?

Open the trip actions menu and choose Duplicate Trip for a new unchecked copy. Reset Checks clears the current trip instead, while Archive Trip moves it out of the active list.

How do I add something the template missed?

Open the trip, tap Add Item, choose its section, enter the item, and save. Custom items behave like every item that came with the template.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings and choose Remove Ads. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Use Restore Purchases on another device signed into the same Apple Account.

Why am I not seeing ads?

Packed Yet? intentionally shows no ads during the first session, in restricted regions, or when the region is unknown. It also limits interstitials to natural breaks and caps how often they appear.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Open Settings and tap Report an Ad, then email support@dudleyapps.com with a screenshot and the approximate time it appeared. Never tap an ad just to identify it.

How do I contact support?

Email support@dudleyapps.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, the screen you were on, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps.

Still need a hand?

Email support@dudleyapps.com, or use thecontact form — either way, it reaches the same inbox and we'll get back to you.
