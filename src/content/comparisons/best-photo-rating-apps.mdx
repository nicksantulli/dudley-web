## The best photo rating apps for iPhone in 2026

Photo rating apps split into two camps: serious critique tools that grade your composition and lighting, and funny "vibe" or "aura" readers that exist to be screenshotted and sent to the group chat. We've compared the most notable options on iPhone honestly — what each is genuinely good at, and which to pick depending on whether you want feedback or a laugh.

## Our pick

For useful, constructive photo feedback, **VibeRate – AI Photo Rating** is the most practical. For a funny read, **Aura Check** wins on roast comedy and **AuraMe** on aesthetic-by-aesthetic scoring. **VibeRater Social** stands apart by turning the rating into a full social loop: post to Vibes, make a Vibie, ask for a VibeCheck, or share with your Squad. If you want the photo rating to start a conversation instead of ending at a scorecard, it is the strongest social pick.
