## What is Table Talk: Conversation Cards?

Table Talk: Conversation Cards is a free iPhone app of hand-written conversation-starter prompts — 420 of them, 70 per category across six social categories, plus a separate 80-card Would You Rather deck — designed for dinner tables, first dates, friend groups, and work teams. Swipe through cards, bookmark your favorites, and share them as images to Stories or group chats. No paywall, no accounts, no internet connection required.

## How Table Talk works

1. **Tap a category** — Date Night, Family Dinner, Friend Group, Work Team, Deep Questions, Light & Fun, or the Would You Rather deck.
2. **Swipe through cards.** Each card is a full-bleed prompt on a color background. Swipe left for the next, right to go back.
3. **Bookmark the ones that hit.** Heart a card to save it — up to 50 favorites. Open Bookmarks and tap Shuffle My Picks to run your personal deck.
4. **Share or screenshot.** Every prompt generates a 1080×1080 share image — your card on the category color. Drop it into a group chat, a story, or a screenshot.

The Daily Featured card rotates once per calendar day, with a distinct pick for every day of the year, determined by your device date — no internet, no server.

## The six categories, plus Would You Rather

- **Date Night** — vulnerability-forward questions that go somewhere. Not "what's your favorite movie." More like "what's something you used to be embarrassed about that you now love about yourself?"
- **Family Dinner** — cross-generational, nostalgic, inviting. Works for everyone at the table, including the grandparents.
- **Friend Group** — playful, slightly roast-able. Zero stakes, high comedy potential.
- **Work Team** — icebreaker-safe, no oversharing, appropriate for a Monday standup.
- **Deep Questions** — substantive without being therapy. The prompts that actually change the direction of a dinner.
- **Light & Fun** — absurd hypotheticals. Zero-stakes scenarios with no wrong answers and a lot of strong opinions.
- **Would You Rather** — 80 either-or cards for when the table wants something quicker. Both options on screen, everyone commits, then argue about it.

## Why Table Talk?

Every competitor in this category either paywalls prompts after the first three, runs a subscription, or has design that makes you want to put your phone face-down at the table. Table Talk charges nothing, paywalls nothing, and keeps ads out of the way — never during a session, never on your first sitting, and at most once a session. The conversation about the app IS the conversation starter. That's the whole trick.

Table Talk is free on the App Store for iPhone.
