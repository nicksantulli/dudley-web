## What is EconByte: Daily Economics?

EconByte is a free iPhone app that explains economics in bite-sized daily cards — plain-language, sourced, and designed to be read in about five minutes. Work through a daily set of 8 cards on inflation, interest rates, GDP, trade, and more. Every card has a concept on the front and a sourced real-world example on the back, citing the institution and document it came from. Build a streak. Actually understand what the Fed is doing and why it matters to your wallet.

For educational purposes only — not financial or investment advice.

## How EconByte works

1. **Start today's set** — 8 cards, about five minutes.
2. **Tap a card to flip it.** Each card has a plain-language concept on the front. Flip to see a sourced real-world example with a real number and a named source — the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Federal Reserve, the Congressional Budget Office, and their peers.
3. **Bookmark anything you want to come back to**, and revisit it later in Review Saved.
4. **Turn on an optional daily reminder** any time in Settings — it's off until you turn it on.

Inflation and Interest Rates are free forever, no account or sign-up required. The other 13 topics — GDP, Supply & Demand, Labor Markets, Trade & Tariffs, Housing Market, Central Banks, Recessions, Debt & Deficits, Exchange Rates, Consumer Spending, Taxes, Fiscal Policy, and Economic Indicators — open together with a single $0.99 Unlock All Topics purchase.

All 120 cards are bundled in the app — no account, no internet, no server required once you're past the one-time purchase flow.

## What you'll learn

EconByte covers 15 economic topics, each with 8 cards, for 120 cards total:

**Free:** Inflation · Interest Rates

**Unlocked together with Unlock All Topics ($0.99):** GDP · Supply & Demand · Labor Markets · Trade & Tariffs · Housing Market · Central Banks · Recessions · Debt & Deficits · Exchange Rates · Consumer Spending · Taxes · Fiscal Policy · Economic Indicators

Every card ends with a source citation and date — the document was checked before the claim shipped, and unverifiable claims were removed rather than estimated. All cards carry the disclaimer: *For educational purposes only — not financial or investment advice.*

## Why EconByte?

Most economics education is a textbook or a podcast you never finish. EconByte is the version that fits in the time between two subway stops: one concept, one sourced example, one number you'll actually remember. It's not trying to make you an economist. It's trying to make you the person at the table who understands what the Fed rate decision means for their mortgage.

EconByte is free on the App Store for iPhone now, with two free topics forever and the rest unlockable with a single $0.99 purchase.
