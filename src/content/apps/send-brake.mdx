## One draft, one clear next step

SendBrake is for the moment before you send an email, text, or post. Paste a draft or bring it in through the iOS share sheet. Get one of three suggestions: Send, Rewrite, or Leave unsent. You make the final call; the app cannot send the message or write one for you.

The default check works offline on your iPhone. Optional Jev makes a closed-set choice online after disclosure. If it cannot finish, the result is clearly labeled as an on-device fallback.

History keeps only the decision and optional short label, not the source message. There is no account, cloud sync, subscription, or generative rewrite.
