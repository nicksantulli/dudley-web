## The Last Human Workplace Arcade

The bots took over the office. Everyone's been replaced.

You're the last human. And you're next.

**Last Human** is a top-down arcade dodge game where you play as the last remaining office worker in an AI-automated workplace. Dodge the bots, grab a power-up, and reach the time clock before the shift timer hits zero. Don't get "optimized."

### How to Play

- Swipe to move through a top-down office floor plan while patrol bots, Rogue Roombas, enhanced bots, meeting bots, and hunters close in
- Grab coffee for speed, a stress ball for brief invincibility, a USB drive for an extra life, or a shield for one free hit
- Reach the time clock before the shift timer expires
- Read the inter-level "corporate memos" — official announcements about how the robots are "improving efficiency"
- Climb 50 increasingly large and maze-like floors, from Reception to Termination Bay
- Earn up to three stars per floor by finishing cleanly and beating the score target

### The Satire

This is a game about AI job displacement, told as a retro arcade experience. Cartoon robots, office humor, and corporate-speak collide. No real companies, no real people — just vibes and bots.

### The Whole Game, Offline

- No account, sign-up, subscription, or energy timer
- Plays offline; progress and best scores stay on your device
- Ads appear only between floors, never during play
- A one-time Remove Ads purchase removes between-floor ads
- One optional watch-to-continue ad is available per run
