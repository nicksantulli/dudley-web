faq:
  - q: "What is En Voz Alta?"
    a: "En Voz Alta is a practice tool for the U.S. citizenship interview. A practice officer asks the civics questions published by USCIS, and you answer out loud in English, the way you will at the interview. Explanations are available in Spanish or English. It is a practice tool, not legal advice. Not affiliated with USCIS or any government agency."
  - q: "Which civics test does it use?"
    a: "Both lists published by USCIS: the 2025 test (128 questions) and the 2008 test (100 questions). The app asks the date you filed your N-400 and shows the test that goes with that date. The 2025 test is for N-400s filed on or after October 20, 2025."
  - q: "Is my voice recorded?"
    a: "No. Your voice is never recorded. The app tells iPhone to recognize speech on the device only, and what you said is erased when the round ends. If English dictation isn't set up on your iPhone, you can say the answer out loud and check yourself, or type."
  - q: "What is free, and what does Pro add?"
    a: "Free: all civics questions, listening practice, English reading and writing practice, 2 practice rounds a day, and a first look at the N-400 questions. Pro, one payment of $14.99 with no subscription: unlimited full mock interviews, the full N-400 practice, a review of every answer after the round with the USCIS wording, and progress by topic. Family Sharing is on."
  - q: "Can the app help with my application or legal questions?"
    a: "No. It is a practice tool, not legal advice, and it does not review forms or tell anyone whether they qualify. For legal questions, talk to a licensed immigration attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative at a recognized organization."
support:
  - q: "How do I turn on English dictation so the app can check my spoken answers?"
    a: "To check spoken answers on this iPhone, iPhone needs English (US) dictation. In iPhone Settings, go to General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard and add English (US). Then, in General > Keyboard, turn on Enable Dictation. Wait a few minutes on Wi-Fi while iPhone downloads it. Your Spanish keyboard stays. Until then, you can still practice out loud and check yourself, or type."
  - q: "I said no to the microphone or speech recognition. Can I still practice?"
    a: "Yes. Every exercise has Check myself and Type instead. With Check myself, you answer out loud, tap Show the answer, then mark I said it or Not yet. To use spoken checks later, open iPhone Settings, scroll to En Voz Alta, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition."
  - q: "How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?"
    a: "Open Settings in the app, go to Pro, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Your practice data is not included in iCloud Backup, so practice progress starts fresh on a new phone."
  - q: "Does Pro work with Family Sharing?"
    a: "Yes. Family Sharing is on for Pro. If someone in your Apple Family Sharing group bought it and has Purchase Sharing turned on, open Settings > Pro in the app. It shows Shared with you through Family Sharing. If it doesn't, tap Restore Purchases."
  - q: "I think an answer in the app is wrong. How do I report it?"
    a: "Open the question and tap Report a wrong answer. It opens an email to support@dudleyapps.com with the question number, the test version, and the app version filled in, and none of your answers. We check every report against the lists published by USCIS. Confirmed errors are fixed in an app update within 7 days."
  - q: "How do I erase my practice data?"
    a: "Open Settings in the app and tap Erase practice data. After one confirmation, it deletes your practice results, dates, state, and the names you typed from this iPhone, and the app starts over. This can't be undone. Deleting the app also removes the data. Pro stays with your Apple Account and can be restored."
