## A packing list that gets out of the way

The awkward part of packing is not writing a list. It is wondering whether the list forgot something. Packed Yet? starts each trip with a useful, ordinary checklist and makes it quick to shape around where you are actually going.

Choose Weekend, Business, Beach, Camping, International, or Family. Give the trip a name and date. Then add what is personal, remove what is not, and check each thing as it goes into the bag.

## Built for the return trip

Good packing lists get reused. Duplicate an old trip for a clean copy, reset the checks when plans repeat, or archive finished trips so they stay available without crowding the next departure.

The list lives on your iPhone. There is no account to create, no packing profile to maintain, and no trip content uploaded to Dudley Development.

## Free without turning packing into an ad break

All six templates and the full checklist workflow are free. A small banner can appear after a trip exists, and an occasional interstitial is limited to natural breaks such as completing, sharing, or archiving a trip. There are no interstitials in the first session, no ads in restricted or unknown regions, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase removes advertising permanently.
