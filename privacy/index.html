Privacy

Effective date: September 7, 2026 · One policy per app — pick yours below.

Dudley Development, LLC makes several separate apps, and they don't all collect the same things, so there isn't one policy that's accurate for all of them. Read the policy for the app you actually use — the table below is a quick summary, and each app's own page has the full detail. A few things are true everywhere: we never sell your personal data, we don't share data across our own apps to build a profile of you, none of our apps are directed at children, and any usage analytics we run ourselves is anonymous and disclosed on the app's page below. Some free apps show ads through Google AdMob, which is disclosed per app, including whether the ad requests are personalized and whether Apple's tracking permission is involved.

What's true across every Dudley app

  • Dudley Development, LLC is the developer of every app listed here. We are not a data broker and we do not sell personal data.
  • None of our apps use your data from one Dudley app to target or personalize another.
  • None of our apps are directed at children, and none knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (VibeRater requires users to be at least 16).
  • Where an app runs its own crash reporting or usage analytics, it is disclosed on that app's page, uses a random per-install or per-account identifier rather than your name or contact details, and is never used to serve ads.
