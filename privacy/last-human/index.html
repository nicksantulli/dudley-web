Privacy Policy

Last Human: Dodge the Bots · Effective date: September 25, 2026

Last Human is available on the App Store.This policy describes version 1.0.1. Version 1.0 never asked for tracking permission and requested only non-personalized ads; everything else below applies to both versions.

The short version

Dudley Development, LLC operates Last Human, a free arcade game supported by ads. There are no accounts, and your progress, high scores, settings, and purchase state stay on your device. The app sends limited anonymous usage analytics through PostHog and anonymous crash reports through Sentry. Google AdMob serves ads. They are personalized only if you allow tracking when Apple's permission prompt appears; otherwise they are non-personalized. The app's own analytics and crash reports are never used to track you.

What stays on your device

  • Your unlocked floors, stars, high scores, and other gameplay progress.
  • Your app settings, analytics preference, and Remove Ads purchase state.

This information is stored locally on your device. We do not receive your score, progress map, or anything you type. Deleting the app removes its local app data from your device; App Store purchase records remain with Apple so eligible purchases can be restored.

Usage analytics (PostHog), with a Settings switch

Last Human sends a small set of product events: app opened, floor started, completed, or failed, Remove Ads purchased or restored, and Settings opened. The events use bounded values such as a floor number, attempt-count range, star count, and failure reason. They do not include free text, exact scores, your progress map, transaction IDs, advertising identifiers, or contact information.

  • Analytics uses a random, app-specific installation ID shown in Settings. It is not tied to a Dudley account because Last Human has no accounts.
  • If you have never set the analytics switch yourself, your answer to Apple's tracking prompt sets it the first time you see the prompt: Allow turns analytics on, and Ask App Not to Track turns it off. You can change it in Settings at any time either way.
  • Turn off Share anonymous usage analytics in Settings to stop new events, clear the unsent queue on your device, and discard that ID. Turning analytics back on creates a new ID.
  • Sent events are retained for 90 days as individual records and up to13 months as aggregated statistics. To request deletion of sent events, copy the Analytics ID before turning analytics off and emailsupport@dudleyapps.com.

Crash reporting (Sentry)

If Last Human crashes, Sentry can receive an anonymous crash report containing the stack trace and coarse technical details such as the app version, iOS major version, device class, and lifecycle state. Crash reporting does not include a user profile, screenshots, view hierarchy, gameplay progress, scores, or advertising state. Crash reports are retained for30 days.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Interstitial ads can appear on the corporate memo screen between floors, never during gameplay. One optional rewarded ad can also let you continue a run. Ads are served byGoogle AdMob, a third-party advertising network.

  • Apple's tracking prompt. Shortly after you first open the app, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether Last Human may track you. No ad is requested until you answer. If you choose Allow, ads may be personalized using your device's advertising identifier. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, or tracking is restricted on your device, every ad request is non-personalized with restricted data processing.
  • Change your answer any time in Settings > Privacy > Manage tracking in the app, which opens Last Human's page in iOS Settings, or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The next ad request follows the new answer.
  • No ads are requested at all in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland. If your region cannot be determined, ads are non-personalized.

Either way, Google may process device, coarse location, ad-interaction, performance, and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads. Dudley does not receive the advertising identifier, and does not join ad data with the app's analytics or use it to build a profile of you.

  • How Google uses this data is described inGoogle's advertising policy andGoogle's privacy policy.
  • A one-time Remove Ads purchase at the price shown in the app removes between-floor interstitial ads and makes the once-per-run revive free, with no ad to watch.

Used to track you

Only if you allow tracking: Google AdMob may then use your device's advertising identifier and ad-interaction data to personalize and measure ads across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, nothing is used to track you. The app's own analytics and crash reports are never used to track you, never linked to your identity, and never combined with data from a data broker. We do not sell your personal data.

Children

Last Human is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. The app has no accounts, profiles, chat, or in-app messaging.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date above.

Contact
