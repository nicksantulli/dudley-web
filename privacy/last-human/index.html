Privacy Policy

Last Human: Dodge the Bots · Effective date: September 21, 2026

Last Human is available on the App Store.This policy describes version 1.0 as shipped.

The short version

Dudley Development, LLC operates Last Human, a free arcade game supported by ads. There are no accounts, and your progress, high scores, settings, and purchase state stay on your device. The app sends limited anonymous usage analytics through PostHog and anonymous crash reports through Sentry. Google AdMob serves non-personalized ads. None of these services is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your device

  • Your unlocked floors, stars, high scores, and other gameplay progress.
  • Your app settings, analytics preference, and Remove Ads purchase state.

This information is stored locally on your device. We do not receive your score, progress map, or anything you type. Deleting the app removes its local app data from your device; App Store purchase records remain with Apple so eligible purchases can be restored.

Usage analytics (PostHog) — on by default, Settings opt-out

Last Human sends a small set of product events: app opened, floor started, completed, or failed, Remove Ads purchased or restored, and Settings opened. The events use bounded values such as a floor number, attempt-count range, star count, and failure reason. They do not include free text, exact scores, your progress map, transaction IDs, advertising identifiers, or contact information.

  • Analytics uses a random, app-specific installation ID shown in Settings. It is not tied to a Dudley account because Last Human has no accounts.
  • Turn off Share anonymous usage analytics in Settings to stop new events, clear the unsent queue on your device, and discard that ID. Turning analytics back on creates a new ID.
  • Sent events are retained for 90 days as individual records and up to13 months as aggregated statistics. To request deletion of sent events, copy the Analytics ID before turning analytics off and emailsupport@dudleyapps.com.

Crash reporting (Sentry)

If Last Human crashes, Sentry can receive an anonymous crash report containing the stack trace and coarse technical details such as the app version, iOS major version, device class, and lifecycle state. Crash reporting does not include a user profile, screenshots, view hierarchy, gameplay progress, scores, or advertising state. Crash reports are retained for30 days.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Interstitial ads can appear on the corporate memo screen between floors, never during gameplay. One optional rewarded ad can also let you continue a run. Ads are served byGoogle AdMob, a third-party advertising network. Last Human requestsnon-personalized ads, does not request Apple's cross-app tracking permission, and does not request ads in the EEA or UK. Google may still process device, coarse location, ad-interaction, performance, and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads. Dudley does not join that information with the app's analytics or use it to build a profile of you.

  • How Google uses this data is described inGoogle's advertising policy andGoogle's privacy policy.
  • A one-time Remove Ads purchase at the price shown in the app removes between-floor interstitial ads. The optional watch-to-continue ad remains available once per run.

Used to track you

None of the data described on this page is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites, linked to your identity, or combined with data from a data broker. We do not sell your personal data.

Children

Last Human is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. The app has no accounts, profiles, chat, or in-app messaging.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date above.

Contact

Questions or a data request? Email support@dudleyapps.com.
