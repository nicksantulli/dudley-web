Privacy Policy

ClaimDesk · Last updated September 25, 2026

This policy describes ClaimDesk version 1.0 and later.

The short version

ClaimDesk keeps track of claims you have already started (insurance, warranty, chargeback): who it's with, the reference number, its status, the next date you need to act, and a log of what happened. Everything stays on your iPhone. Dudley Development, LLC doesn't receive your claims. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • The claims you add and the touches you log, including any proof notes.
  • Your reminder permission and, if you set one up, your connector address and device token (the token is kept in the iOS Keychain).

Deleting the app removes all of it.

The optional connector

If you set up a ClaimDesk connector in Settings, your phone sends your saved claims and touch logs to the address you enter, and nowhere else. The connector is software you, or someone you choose, run; it isn't operated by Dudley Development, LLC. Only apps you approve on that connector can read those claims, set a claim to "waiting" or "needs me", log touches, and ask to close a claim (you confirm the close in the app). Removing the connector in Settings stops syncing; data already on the connector stays wherever you run it.

Share sheet

When you share something into ClaimDesk it records a short note: the link, the start of the text, or a file's name and SHA-256 fingerprint. It doesn't copy the file.

Reminders

Next-action reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone, and only if you allow notifications. No push server is involved.

Purchases

ClaimDesk Pro, a one-time purchase, lets you track more than one open claim. Apple processes payment through StoreKit; we never see your payment details. Purchases can be restored from Settings.

Used to track you

Nothing. ClaimDesk has no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell personal data.

Children

ClaimDesk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date above.
