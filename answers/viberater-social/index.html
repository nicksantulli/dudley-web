VibeRater Social

Rate the pic. Post the Vibie. Find your people.

VibeRater Social is a camera-to-social loop for daily vibe checks, Squad reactions, MiniVibe, Radar, and cleaner feeds.

What is VibeRater Social?

VibeRater Social is the next social version of VibeRater: rate a photo, turn it into a Vibie, share it with a Squad, and discover people or places by vibe rather than follower count.

Can I download VibeRater Social today?

You can download VibeRater: Aura today on the App Store. VibeRater Social is the next version, built around Vibies, Squad reactions, Radar, MiniVibe, and cleaner feeds.

What are Vibies?

Vibies are story-style posts made from rated photos, captions, receipts, share cards, or anthem-style moments.

What is SlopGuard?

SlopGuard is a cleaner-feed helper for suspected AI-generated image posts. It is a filter aid, not a promise that every AI image will be detected perfectly.

Does VibeRater sell my personal data?

No. Dudley does not sell your personal data. For the app available today, the VibeRater privacy policy explains how photos and app data are handled.
