What Is a Vibie?

Dudley Development · July 4, 2026

A Vibie is the VibeRater Social idea of turning a rated photo into a social post. Here's how it fits with VibeRater, Squad reactions, VibeChecks, and Radar.

VibeRaterVibeRater SocialAnswersVibe Check Apps

A Vibie is the VibeRater Social concept for turning a rated photo into a social post. The phrase is intentionally simple: Rate the pic. Post the Vibie. Find your people.

VibeRater: Aura is the app available today. It rates photos and creates shareable vibe cards. VibeRater Social is the v2 direction, where those ratings become more social through Vibies, Squad reactions, VibeChecks, Radar, MiniVibe, and a cleaner feed.

The short answer

A Vibie is not just a normal photo post. It starts with a vibe read.

That means the post can carry:

  • The photo
  • The vibe score
  • The archetype
  • The aura or mood
  • A caption, receipt, anthem, or share card
  • Reactions from friends

The goal is to make posting feel less like performance and more like a daily vibe check.

Why call it a Vibie?

The name makes the product behavior obvious. You rate a picture. The rated picture becomes a Vibie. Friends can react to the vibe, not just the image.

That matters because most social apps flatten everything into the same format: photo, caption, likes, comments. VibeRater Social is aiming at a different prompt. It asks, “what is the energy today?”

How Vibies fit with VibeRater: Aura

The current app, VibeRater: Aura, is already built around shareable results. You choose a selfie, regular photo, squad shot, or pet pic. The app gives you a scorecard, grade, archetype, aura color, and card.

A Vibie is the natural next step: instead of saving the result only as a card, the result can become a post inside VibeRater Social.

How Vibies fit with Squad and VibeChecks

VibeRater Social is not just about posting into the void. The ASC v2 direction includes:

  • Squad: your friend circle, reactions, and daily energy comparisons
  • VibeChecks: friends answering your vibe with their own selfie reaction
  • Radar: nearby places, events, and people with similar-vibe discovery
  • MiniVibe: a tiny companion that reacts to your daily vibe
  • SlopGuard: filtering suspected AI-generated image posts from the feed

Vibies are the post format that holds the daily moment. Squad and VibeChecks are how friends respond.

Is this live today?

Not as a full social product yet. The App Store app available today is VibeRater: Aura. It rates photos, creates share cards, and is live on iPhone.

VibeRater Social is the direction for v2. We are keeping that distinction clear on purpose: users can download VibeRater today, while the social language points to where the product is going.
