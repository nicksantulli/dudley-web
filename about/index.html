About Dudley Development

Independent iOS studio · Dudley Development, LLC · United States

Dudley Development is a small independent studio making iPhone apps — the kind that are quick to pick up, do one thing with a bit of personality, and don't ask anything of you to start playing.

What we believe

  • Free to download. Every app is free on the App Store. Paid upgrades like Remove Ads are optional — the core experience ships unlocked.
  • Low-friction by default. You can start using our apps immediately after download. If a social feature needs a profile, we explain what it stores and give you deletion controls.
  • Privacy by feature. Each privacy policy says what stays on-device, what reaches a service, what is stored, and why.
  • Honest about ads. Our free apps are ad-supported through Google AdMob and disclosed plainly. The ads are requested as non-personalized, and our apps do not request cross-app tracking permission.
  • Polish is the point. Tight controls, real haptics, and a sense of humor — details that make an app feel made, not generated.
