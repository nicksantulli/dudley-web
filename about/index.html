About Dudley Development

Dudley Development, LLC · Founded by Nicholas Santulli · United States

Dudley Development builds iPhone products you can understand before you download: games, conversation starters, social tools, and short lessons with clear prices and privacy boundaries.

How the apps work

  • Download price. Every Dudley app is free on the App Store. Optional purchases, when offered, are described on the app page.
  • Accounts. Most apps work without one. VibeRater uses profiles for its social features and provides account-deletion controls.
  • Privacy. Each app page states what works offline, what stays on the device, and where to read the full policy.
  • Ads. Ad-supported apps request non-personalized ads and do not request Apple’s cross-app tracking permission.
