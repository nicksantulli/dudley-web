The Last Human Workplace Arcade

The bots took over the office. Everyone’s been replaced.

You’re the last human. And you’re next.

Last Human is a top-down arcade dodge game where you play as the last remaining office worker in an AI-automated workplace. Dodge the bots before they take your desk. Survive as long as you can. Don’t get “optimized.”

How to Play

  • Move your character to dodge waves of robots rolling in from every direction
  • Clear each department (Marketing, Engineering, HR, the C-Suite…) to advance
  • Read the inter-level “corporate memos” — official announcements about how the robots are “improving efficiency”
  • Beat 10 levels of escalating robot density
  • Chase your high score across runs

The Satire

This is a game about AI job displacement, told as a retro arcade experience. Cartoon robots, office humor, and corporate-speak collide. No real companies, no real people — just vibes and bots.

Simple to Learn, Hard to Put Down

  • One finger or tilt controls
  • 10 levels, each themed to a corporate department
  • 0–3 star rating per level
  • Persistent high score
  • Cross-promote to Powell Prowl — the only other Dudley app you might actually need

Frequently asked questions

What is Last Human?

Last Human is a satirical arcade game about AI job displacement. You play as the last office worker, dodging robot waves across 10 themed departments (Marketing, Engineering, HR, the C-Suite). Each level has escalating difficulty, department flavor, and a comedic 'corporate memo' between rounds. It's free to play with a one-time $0.99 option to remove ads.

Is Last Human free?

Yes. Last Human is free to download and play. An interstitial ad appears between levels (styled as corporate memos). You can remove ads forever with a one-time $0.99 in-app purchase.

How much does the ad-free version cost?

The Remove Ads in-app purchase is $0.99 (USD). This is a one-time purchase that removes interstitials for the life of the app on your device.

What platforms does Last Human support?

Last Human is available on iPhone (iOS 16.6 or later). It requires one-finger or tilt controls and works on any compatible iPhone.

How many levels does Last Human have?

The current version includes 10 levels, each themed to a different corporate department. The difficulty escalates, and each level has 0–3 star ratings based on your performance.
