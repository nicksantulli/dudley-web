One draft, one clear next step

SendBrake is for the moment before you send an email, text, or post. Paste a draft or bring it in through the iOS share sheet. Get one of three suggestions: Send, Rewrite, or Leave unsent. You make the final call; the app cannot send the message or write one for you.

The default check works offline on your iPhone. Optional Jev makes a closed-set choice online after disclosure. If it cannot finish, the result is clearly labeled as an on-device fallback.

History keeps only the decision and optional short label, not the source message. There is no account, cloud sync, subscription, or generative rewrite.

Frequently asked questions

What does SendBrake do?

It checks one draft before you send it and suggests Send, Rewrite, or Leave unsent. You can change the result. It is a decision aid, not a guarantee, and it never sends for you.

Does it read my inbox or save the draft?

No. It has no inbox connection. History saves only the decision card, never the draft, note, or original URL.

Does Jev rewrite my message?

No. Jev returns one closed-set choice. The optional online check sends the draft to TypeSafe AI after disclosure; it does not generate wording.

Is it free?

Yes. The core check is free. Restrained ads support it where allowed, and a one-time $2.99 purchase removes them.
