VibeRater Social

Your vibe, your people, your terms

Live on the App Store

What is VibeRater Social?

VibeRater Social is a free iPhone social app for sharing the moments that already belong in your group chat — and for building an entire vibe around them. Rate a selfie, outfit, pet, group shot, or completely random moment with the VibeMeter, then decide what happens next: keep it private, post it to Vibes, turn it into a VibeRodeo, or share it with your Squad. Beyond ratings, VibeRater is also home to your Repli — a customizable avatar and room you can decorate, shop for, and visit with friends.

From photo to vibe

  1. Pick a photo or take a new one.
  2. Let the VibeMeter turn it into a 0-to-100 score with an aura-style read.
  3. Turn it into a VibeRodeo — trim clips, control photo timing, add filters, stickers, sound, and transitions — or keep it a simple photo post.
  4. Keep the result private, post it to Vibes, or share it directly with your Squad.

The rating is the spark, not the finish line. VibeRater turns that result into a conversation while leaving you in control of where it appears.

What’s live now

  • VibeRodeo turns photos and short clips into a video post, with a dedicated photo editor for stills and full editing tools — trimming, frame timing, text, captions, filters, drawing, stickers, sound, and transitions — for video.
  • Repli is your customizable avatar and room. Style your look, decorate your space, add rooms, choose a pet, browse VibeShop for items you can try before buying, and use Play for activities or fictional food delivery. Visit eligible friends’ rooms too.
  • Following is one-way, so you can keep up with a public profile without a mutual Squad friendship — private profiles and follow requests stay in your control.
  • Feeds switch between Full Screen and Timeline, covering your Squad, recommendations, and nearby activity, with search across people, posts, places, and hashtags.
  • Radar surfaces nearby places and events and lets you turn discovery into an actual plan you can invite people to.
  • Squad, Vibes, Vibie story-style posts, VibeChecks, and Rise (with its daily Vibescope) are all still part of the toolkit.
  • SlopGuard is an optional setting that can filter suspected AI-generated image posts from your feed.
  • New in the app: clean, contextual tips introduce these features over time and can be snoozed or replayed.

Your vibe, your terms

A rating starts private. You choose whether to post it, share it with your Squad, or keep it to yourself. Photos used only for a rating are processed in memory and discarded; media you deliberately post, share, or save is stored so those features work. VibeRater does not sell your personal information, and SlopGuard is optional.

VibeRater is deliberately for fun. The scores, auras, archetypes, and Vibescopes are not measurements of appearance, personality, health, or any personal trait. Come for the vibe check; stay for the Repli room, the Squad, and the people who actually get your energy.

Frequently asked questions

What is VibeRater Social?

VibeRater Social is a free iPhone social app built around playful photo ratings and social discovery. Rate a selfie, outfit, pet, group shot, or everyday photo with the VibeMeter, then keep the result private, share it with your Squad, or post it to Vibes. Beyond ratings, it's also a place to create VibeRodeo video posts, build a Repli avatar and room, browse VibeShop, and follow people one-way.

How does VibeRater Social rate your vibe?

Pick or take a photo and the VibeMeter gives it a playful score from 0 to 100 with an aura-style read. It's entertainment and creative expression — not a biometric, health, identity, attractiveness, or clinical measurement of you.

What is a VibeRodeo?

A VibeRodeo turns photos and short clips into a video post. Trim and arrange clips, control photo timing, add text, captions, filters, drawing, stickers, sound, and transitions, then save a draft or publish. A dedicated photo editor keeps the tools that make sense for still images.

What is a Repli?

Your Repli is a customizable avatar you can style and use to decorate a room. You can add rooms, choose a pet, try items from VibeShop before buying them, and use Play for activities or fictional food delivery. You can also visit eligible friends' rooms.

How is Following different from Squad?

Squad stays reciprocal — it's your inner circle. Following is one-way, so you can keep up with a public profile without sending or accepting a friend request. Private profiles stay in control of who can follow them, and report, block, and remove-follower tools are built in.

What is SlopGuard?

SlopGuard is an optional feed setting that can hide non-rated image posts flagged as suspected AI-generated. It's designed to make the feed feel more human, and you control whether it's on in Settings.

Is VibeRater Social accurate?

No. VibeRater is for entertainment only. Its scores, auras, archetypes, and Vibescopes are playful outputs, not measurements of appearance, personality, health, or any personal trait.

Does VibeRater Social upload my photos?

A photo you only rate is sent to the rating service, processed in memory, and discarded. If you choose to post, share, or save content, that selected media and its related social data are stored so those features work. VibeRater does not sell your personal information.

Is VibeRater Social free?

Yes. VibeRater Social is free to download. Optional in-app purchases include Remove Ads, fixed VibeBoost credits, and fixed-quantity VibeToken packs used for post boosts. They have no cash value, prizes, or randomized rewards.
