VibeRater Social

Your vibe, your people, your terms · Live on the App Store

What is VibeRater Social?

VibeRater Social is a free iPhone social app for sharing the moments that already belong in your group chat. Rate a selfie, outfit, pet, group shot, or completely random moment with the VibeMeter, then decide what happens next: keep it private, post it to your Vibes, make it a Vibie, send it to your Squad, or share it anywhere.

From photo to vibe

  1. Pick a photo or take a new one.
  2. Let the VibeMeter turn it into a 0-to-100 score, aura-style read, archetype, and vibe anthem.
  3. Make a story-ready share card, receipt, or anthem-style post.
  4. Keep the result private, post it to Vibes, or share it directly with your Squad.

The rating is the spark, not the finish line. VibeRater v2.0 turns that result into a conversation while leaving you in control of where it appears.

New in VibeRater v2.0

  • Vibes is the main feed for ratings and everyday moments, with comments, likes, quotes, and reactions.
  • Vibies turn rated photos into quick, story-style posts.
  • VibeChecks invite friends to answer your vibe with a selfie reaction.
  • Squad keeps your inner circle together and compares everyone’s daily energy side by side.
  • Radar surfaces nearby places, events, and people giving off a compatible vibe.
  • Rise is your daily corner: a just-for-fun Vibescope, shareable rituals, streaks, Today’s Move, and Fit Files.
  • SlopGuard is an optional setting that can filter suspected AI-generated image posts from your feed.

Your vibe, your terms

A rating starts private. You choose whether to post it, share it with your Squad, or keep it to yourself. Photos used only for a rating are processed in memory and discarded; media you deliberately post, share, or save is stored so those features work. VibeRater does not sell your personal information, and SlopGuard is optional.

VibeRater is deliberately for fun. The scores, auras, archetypes, anthems, and Vibescopes are not measurements of appearance, personality, health, or any personal trait. Come for the vibe check; stay for the people who actually get your energy.

Frequently asked questions

What is VibeRater Social?

VibeRater Social is a free iPhone social app built around playful photo ratings and real connection. Rate a selfie, outfit, pet, group shot, or random moment, then keep it private, post it to Vibes, turn it into a Vibie, or share it with your Squad.

How does VibeRater Social rate your vibe?

Pick or take a photo and the VibeMeter gives it a playful score from 0 to 100, an aura-style read, an archetype, and a vibe anthem. Turn the result into a share card, receipt, or anthem-style post. It is entertainment, not analysis or a measurement of you.

What can I do with friends in VibeRater?

Post ratings and moments to the Vibes feed, comment, like, quote, and react; make quick Vibies from rated photos; send a VibeCheck that friends answer with a selfie; and add your closest people to a Squad for a side-by-side daily vibe check.

What is SlopGuard?

SlopGuard is an optional feed setting that can hide non-rated image posts flagged as suspected AI-generated. It is designed to make the feed feel more human, and you control whether it is on.

Is VibeRater Social accurate?

No. VibeRater is for entertainment only. Its scores, auras, archetypes, anthems, and Vibescopes are playful outputs, not measurements of appearance, personality, health, or any personal trait.

Does VibeRater Social upload my photos?

A photo you only rate is sent to the rating service, processed in memory, and discarded. If you choose to post, share, or save content, that selected media and its related social data are stored so those features work. VibeRater does not sell your personal information.

Is VibeRater Social free?

Yes. VibeRater Social is free to download. Optional in-app purchases include Remove Ads, fixed VibeBoost credits, and fixed-quantity VibeToken packs used for post boosts. They have no cash value, prizes, or randomized rewards.
