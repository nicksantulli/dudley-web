What is VibeRater Social?

VibeRater Social is a playful home for photo ratings, creative posts, and social discovery—built around what you choose to share. Rate a selfie, outfit, pet, group shot, or everyday moment, then keep the result private, share it with your Squad, or post it to Vibes.

Rate the moment

  1. Pick a photo or take a new one.
  2. Let the VibeMeter turn it into a 0-to-100 score with an aura-style read.
  3. Make a story-ready share card, receipt, anthem-style post, or VibeCheck.
  4. Choose whether the result stays private, goes to your Squad, or appears in Vibes.

The rating is the spark, not the finish line. VibeRater turns that result into a conversation while leaving you in control of where it appears.

Create and discover in version 2.1

  • Timeline and Full Screen let you move among Squad, Recs, and Nearby without losing your place.
  • VibeRodeo turns photos and short clips into projects with trimming, timing, text, captions, filters, drawing, stickers, sound, transitions, drafts, and format-specific editors.
  • Repli and VibeShop let you create and style a Repli, decorate rooms, try items before buying, choose a pet, visit eligible friends, and use Play for activities or fictional delivery.
  • Radar events turn nearby discovery into plans you can create and join.
  • Following and search make it easier to find people, posts, places, and hashtags while preserving private-profile controls.
  • Contextual feature tips introduce new tools over time and can be snoozed or replayed.

Your vibe, your terms

A rating starts private. You choose whether to post it, share it with your Squad, or keep it to yourself. Photos used only for a rating are processed in memory and discarded; media you deliberately post, share, or save is stored so those features work. VibeRater does not sell your personal information.

VibeRater is deliberately for fun. Its scores, auras, archetypes, anthems, and Vibescopes are not biometric, health, identity, attractiveness, or clinical analysis. Come for the vibe check; stay for the people who actually get your energy.

Frequently asked questions

What is Vibe Rater?

VibeRater Social is a free iPhone social app built around playful photo ratings, creative posts, and real connection. Rate a moment, keep the result private, post it to Vibes, turn it into a Vibie, or share it with your Squad.

How does Vibe Rater rate your vibe?

Pick or take a photo and the VibeMeter gives it a playful score from 0 to 100 with an aura-style read. Ratings are entertainment and creative expression, not biometric, health, identity, attractiveness, or clinical analysis.

What can I do with friends in VibeRater?

Post ratings and moments to Vibes, comment, react, make Vibies, answer VibeChecks, add your closest people to a Squad, or follow public profiles without requiring a mutual friendship.

What is VibeRodeo?

VibeRodeo is VibeRater's creation studio for photos and short clips. Trim and arrange clips, control photo timing, add text, captions, filters, drawing, stickers, sound, and transitions, then save a draft or publish.

What is Repli?

Repli is a customizable companion and room experience. Style your look, decorate and expand rooms, try items before buying them in VibeShop, choose a pet, visit eligible friends, and use Play for activities or fictional delivery.

What is SlopGuard?

SlopGuard is an optional feed setting that can hide non-rated image posts flagged as suspected AI-generated. You control whether it is enabled.

Is Vibe Rater accurate?

No. VibeRater is for entertainment only. Its scores, auras, archetypes, anthems, and Vibescopes are playful outputs, not measurements of appearance, personality, health, identity, or any personal trait.

Does Vibe Rater upload my photos?

A photo used only for a rating is sent to the rating service, processed in memory, and discarded. Media you deliberately post, share, or save is stored so those features work. VibeRater does not sell your personal information.

Is Vibe Rater free?

Yes. VibeRater Social is free to download. Optional purchases include Remove Ads, fixed VibeBoost credits, and fixed-quantity VibeToken packs used for fixed post-boost tiers. They have no cash value, prizes, or randomized rewards.
