Packed Yet? Trip Checklist

A calmer bag starts with a list that already knows the basics.

Packed Yet? is a fast, private packing checklist for iPhone. Start from a practical trip template, tailor the list, check items off, and reuse the trip next time — no account and no cloud setup.

A packing list that gets out of the way

The awkward part of packing is not writing a list. It is wondering whether the list forgot something. Packed Yet? starts each trip with a useful, ordinary checklist and makes it quick to shape around where you are actually going.

Choose Weekend, Business, Beach, Camping, International, or Family. Give the trip a name and date. Then add what is personal, remove what is not, and check each thing as it goes into the bag.

Built for the return trip

Good packing lists get reused. Duplicate an old trip for a clean copy, reset the checks when plans repeat, or archive finished trips so they stay available without crowding the next departure.

The list lives on your iPhone. There is no account to create, no packing profile to maintain, and no trip content uploaded to Dudley Development.

Free without turning packing into an ad break

All six templates and the full checklist workflow are free. A small banner can appear after a trip exists, and an occasional interstitial is limited to natural breaks such as completing, sharing, or archiving a trip. There are no interstitials in the first session, no ads in restricted or unknown regions, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase removes advertising permanently.

Frequently asked questions

What is Packed Yet?

Packed Yet? is a private trip packing checklist for iPhone. Pick a starter template, name the trip, adjust the list, and check things off as they go into the bag.

Is Packed Yet? free?

Yes. The full packing workflow and all six templates are free. The app is supported by restrained ads, with a one-time Remove Ads purchase for $2.99 USD or the equivalent price in your App Store region.

Does it require an account or internet connection?

No. Packed Yet? has no account and stores trip names, dates, and checklist items on your iPhone. Core packing features work offline. An internet connection is only needed for ads, purchases, or opening support links.

What packing templates are included?

Packed Yet? includes six practical starting points: Weekend, Business, Beach, Camping, International, and Family. Every generated list can be edited for the actual trip.

Can I reuse a packing list?

Yes. Duplicate a previous trip to get a fresh unchecked copy, or reset its checks and pack the same list again. Finished trips can be archived instead of deleted.

Does Packed Yet? track me?

Packed Yet? does not create an account, upload your trip content, or request Apple's tracking permission. Ads are requested as non-personalized, and the app makes no ad requests in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or when it cannot determine the region.
