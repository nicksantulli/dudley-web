What is Table Talk: Conversation Cards?

Table Talk: Conversation Cards is a free iPhone app of hand-written conversation-starter prompts: 420 of them, 70 per category across six social categories, plus a separate 80-card Would You Rather deck. It is designed for dinner tables, first dates, friend groups, and work teams. Swipe through cards, bookmark your favorites, and share them as images to Stories or group chats. No account needed, works offline.

How Table Talk works

  1. Tap a category: Date Night, Family Dinner, Friend Group, Work Team, Deep Questions, Light & Fun, or the Would You Rather deck.
  2. Swipe through cards. Each card is a full-bleed prompt on a color background. Swipe left for the next, right to go back.
  3. Bookmark the ones that hit. Heart a card to save it, up to 50 favorites. Open Bookmarks and tap Shuffle My Picks to run your personal deck.
  4. Share or screenshot. Every prompt generates a 1080×1080 share image of your card on the category color. Drop it into a group chat, a story, or a screenshot.

The Daily Featured card rotates once per calendar day, with a distinct pick for every day of the year, determined by your device date. No internet or server is involved.

The six categories, plus Would You Rather

  • Date Night: vulnerability-forward questions that go somewhere. Not “what’s your favorite movie.” More like “what’s something you used to be embarrassed about that you now love about yourself?”
  • Family Dinner: cross-generational, nostalgic, inviting. Works for everyone at the table, including the grandparents.
  • Friend Group: playful, slightly roast-able. Zero stakes, high comedy potential.
  • Work Team: icebreaker-safe, no oversharing, appropriate for a Monday standup.
  • Deep Questions: substantive without being therapy. The prompts that actually change the direction of a dinner.
  • Light & Fun: absurd hypotheticals. Zero-stakes scenarios with no wrong answers and a lot of strong opinions.
  • Would You Rather: 80 either-or cards for when the table wants something quicker. Both options on screen, everyone commits, then argue about it.

Why Table Talk?

Table Talk is free to download, and the six main decks are free with no subscription. Ads stay out of the way: never during a session, never on your first sitting, and at most once a session.

Using Table Talk at the table

Put one phone in the middle

When a pause hits, private phones are an easy exit. One phone face-up in the middle does the opposite: everyone looks at the same card. Pick a category, read the card out loud, and pass the phone on.

Ask for a story, not a résumé

Questions like “what do you do?” ask for a label with a short answer, and then everyone waits for the next question. Better openers ask for a preference, a recent choice, or a small story. Stay with one detail the person mentioned, and stop after two follow-ups so they can ask you something.

Use Would You Rather once, not ten times

A forced choice gives everyone a side. The pick is fast, and the reason behind it is the conversation. Good dilemmas have a real appeal or cost on both sides and are dinner-safe. Skip it when the table is already inside a story. One card between courses is enough.

Keep a holiday dinner off politics

Say early that the night is for the people in the room. When a political comment lands, park it in one sentence, then ask one specific person about a meal, a memory, or a tradition. A question aimed at the whole room lets the same two people restart the fight. Family Dinner is the category built for mixed ages.

Table games with no supplies

For the stretch between dinner and dessert, play one short round everyone can join or pass: a list where each person adds one item and nobody repeats, charades drawn from the meal and the room, or a single Would You Rather. If people are already deep in a story, leave them there.

Frequently asked questions

What is Table Talk: Conversation Cards?

Table Talk: Conversation Cards is a free iPhone app of hand-written conversation-starter prompts for dinner tables, first dates, friend groups, and work teams. Tap a category, swipe through cards, bookmark the ones that go somewhere, and share your favorites. No account needed, and it works offline.

Is Table Talk free?

Yes. All 420 cards in the six main decks and the 80-card Would You Rather deck are free. The Road Trip, Holiday Table and Kids' Table packs, a pack bundle, the Daily Featured Archive and Remove Ads are optional one-time purchases at the prices shown in the app. There is no subscription. The app is supported by ads that appear only as you leave a session, never during one and never on your first sitting.

How much is the Remove Ads purchase?

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It removes all ads from Table Talk permanently, priced by Apple in your storefront's currency. You can restore your purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

What categories does Table Talk have?

Table Talk has six categories, 70 hand-written prompts each, for 420 cards total: Date Night (vulnerability-forward questions), Family Dinner (cross-generational, nostalgic), Friend Group (playful, slightly roast-able), Work Team (icebreaker-safe, no oversharing), Deep Questions (substantive without being therapy), and Light & Fun (absurd hypotheticals, zero stakes). There's also a separate Would You Rather deck of 80 either-or cards.

What is Would You Rather?

Would You Rather is a deck of 80 either-or cards for when the table wants something faster than a full prompt. Both options show on screen, everyone commits to one, then you argue about it. It sits alongside the six main categories.

Does Table Talk require the internet?

No. All prompts are bundled with the app, so there is no download, no account and no internet connection required. The Daily Featured card rotates offline using the date, with a different card for every day of the year. The cards, the daily card and your bookmarks work without an internet connection.

What platform is Table Talk on?

Table Talk runs on iPhone (iOS 16.6 or later) and is live on the App Store.

Does Table Talk show ads?

Ads never interrupt a session and never show on your first sitting. Once you've been playing for a while, at most one full-screen ad can appear as you leave a session, and never more than two in a day. You can also choose to watch a short video to open a prompt pack free for 24 hours; that ad plays only if you tap it. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes ads permanently.
