ActionCard: Next Steps

End the conversation with one next step everyone can repeat.

ActionCard is a fast, private action-item app for iPhone. Capture the next step, owner, and due date; then keep it close or share a clear summary — no account or cloud setup.

The commitment, not another project plan

ActionCard is for the moment when a conversation produces one real next step. Capture the action, name the owner, add the due date if there is one, and move on. The result is a focused card instead of a task buried inside meeting notes.

The dashboard keeps overdue, due-today, and upcoming actions visible. Search finds an action by its wording, owner, or note. Completing, reopening, and duplicating are deliberate, quick operations rather than a new system to maintain.

Share the same understanding

An action can leave the app as a concise text summary or a polished card image through the standard iOS share sheet. Only the content you choose is handed to the destination you choose; Dudley Development does not receive the share.

Local first, with ads kept to the edges

Actions stay on your iPhone. There is no account, sync service, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK. The free app can show a small banner after an action exists and an occasional interstitial after a meaningful completion or share. There are no ad requests in restricted or unknown regions, no full-screen ad in the first session, and a one-time purchase removes ads permanently.

Frequently asked questions

What is ActionCard?

ActionCard turns the end of a meeting, call, or table conversation into one clear commitment: who owns it, what happens next, and when it is due.

Is ActionCard free?

Yes. The full action workflow is free. The app is supported by restrained ads, with a one-time Remove Ads purchase priced in your App Store region.

Does ActionCard require an account or internet connection?

No. Actions are stored locally on your iPhone, and the core app works offline. Internet access is only needed for ads, purchases, or opening support links.

Can I share an action?

Yes. Share a concise text summary or a polished action-card image through the standard iOS share sheet. Dudley Development does not receive a copy.

Does ActionCard track me?

ActionCard does not create an account, upload action content, or request Apple's tracking permission. Ads are requested as non-personalized, and the app makes no ad requests in the EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland, or when it cannot determine the region.
