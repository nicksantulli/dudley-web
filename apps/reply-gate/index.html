ReplyGate: Reply or Skip

Give the message one clear next step, then move on.

Make the decision, not the perfect reply

ReplyGate is for a message that keeps taking up more attention than it deserves. Paste it or share it into the app, answer three quick questions, and leave with one clear outcome: Reply now, Reply later, or Let it go. You can override the suggestion because the final call stays yours.

The result can be saved as a short local history item or shared as a clean decision card. Reply later can schedule an optional local reminder. The original message is excluded from history, reminders, and result cards.

Deliberately disconnected

ReplyGate does not connect to an inbox, read a conversation, send a message, or generate a response. There is no account, cloud sync, collaboration layer, or subscription. The share extension is simply a faster way to hand the text you selected to the same on-device decision gate.

Local first, with ads kept to the edges

Core decisions, history, reminders, and result sharing work offline. Where ads are allowed, the free app can show a small banner after a saved result and an occasional interstitial after a natural exit. There are no ad requests in restricted or unknown regions, no full-screen ad in the first session, and a one-time purchase removes ads.

Frequently asked questions

What is ReplyGate?

ReplyGate is a fast decision gate for a message you are overthinking. Paste or share the text, answer three questions, then decide whether to reply now, reply later, or let it go.

Does ReplyGate connect to my inbox or send messages?

No. It has no Mail, Messages, Slack, or social account connection and cannot send a reply. You choose what to paste or share into it.

Does ReplyGate save the message?

No. The source message is used only for the active decision and is discarded afterward. Saved history keeps only the short label, outcome, rationale code, and dates you choose.

Does ReplyGate write a reply for me?

No. Version 1 does not use generative AI or draft messages. The app helps you make the decision; your words remain yours.

Is ReplyGate free?

Yes. The full decision workflow is free. Restrained ads support the app where they are allowed, and a one-time $2.99 Remove Ads purchase removes them.
