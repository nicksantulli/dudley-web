Where's This House?

Mystery-house map game · Built for HomeLight

Download on theApp StoreProduct support

Guess the house. Pin the map.

You're dropped at a random residential Street View scene somewhere in the United States. Explore the neighborhood, scan for clues, and drop your pin before time runs out. Where's This House? turns each five-round solo run into a quick geography challenge, with distance-and-speed scoring, guess-versus-location results, and a leaderboard view.

What is new in version 2.0?

Native iPad support

Version 2.0 adapts across portrait, landscape, full-screen, and resizable iPad windows.

Larger mystery catalog

Explore an expanded set of approved residential Street View scenes from across the United States.

Five-round solo runs

Play as a guest through five timed houses, then compare every guess with the actual location.

Distance + speed scoring

Closer pins earn more points, with faster accurate guesses adding to the result.

Leaderboard identity

Create an optional player account to claim a handle and support leaderboard attribution.

Refreshed HomeLight cards

Updated informational real-estate cards and reliability improvements round out the release.

What is coming next?

Daily Drop, Play a Friend, and Online Match appear in the app as upcoming experiences and are labeled Soon or Coming Soon. Version 2.0's live playable loop is the five-round solo game.

Residential discovery, without private-home data

No homeowner names, exact addresses, listings, or private transaction data are shown. Rounds use public-road Street View scenes and broad location labels. A player account is optional for claiming a handle and supporting leaderboard attribution, and account deletion is available in the app. Refreshed HomeLight information cards appear between rounds.
