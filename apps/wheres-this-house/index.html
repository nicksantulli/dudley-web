Where's This House?

Mystery-house map game · Built for HomeLight

Download on theApp StoreProduct support

Guess the house. Pin the map.

You're dropped at a random residential Street View scene somewhere in the United States. Explore the neighborhood, scan for clues, and drop your pin before the 90-second clock runs out. Where's This House? keeps the five-round solo game and adds shared daily play, direct challenges, clearer scoring, and competitive boards in version 2.0.

Submitted version 2.0 screens

A completely refreshed game

The name, icon, interface, modes, and scoring experience shown here match the 2.0 App Store submission.

What is new in version 2.0?

Five-house Daily Drops

Everyone gets the same shared mystery run and one daily board.

Challenge a friend

Play the same homes, share a code, and compare scores directly.

Clearer scoring

Earn up to 4,750 distance points plus a speed boost of up to 250 per house.

Server-backed timers

Each 90-second round locks automatically at 0:00 for consistent shared competition.

Leaderboards

Daily and all-time boards show player names, handles, and scores.

Optional notifications

Choose whether to get reminders for shared game activity.

Residential discovery, without private-home data

No homeowner names, exact addresses, listings, or private transaction data are shown. Rounds use public-road Street View scenes and broad location labels. A player account is optional for saved solo scores and required for shared modes. A HomeLight-powered real estate card appears after the final round.
